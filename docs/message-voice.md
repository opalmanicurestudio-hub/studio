# How we write to clients and staff

Every message the app sends on a business's behalf (texts, emails, in-app notices, and the policy wording shown on client pages) should sound like the best person at your front desk: **professional, and human**. Warm, courteous, clear and complete. Never casual or clipped, and never stiff, legal or robotic.

## Professional, and human

- **Write the way a kind, polished person talks.** Contractions are welcome ("we're", "you'll", "it's"). Short sentences. Plain words.
- **Lead with the person, then the policy.** One genuine line of empathy or thanks first, then the facts.
- **Name the policy once, in plain words.** "Because it was within 24 hours of your appointment, our cancellation fee of $32.50 applies." Not "As outlined in our cancellation policy, appointments cancelled less than 24 hours in advance are subject to…"
- **Sound like the same person every time,** but don't repeat the same stock phrase in every message ("We apologize for any inconvenience" everywhere reads like a machine). Vary it: "We're sorry about this.", "We know this isn't the news you were hoping for.", "Thank you for understanding."
- **End warmly and usefully:** what happens next, or how to reach us.

| Stiff | Professional and human |
|---|---|
| As outlined in our cancellation policy, appointments cancelled less than 24 hours in advance are subject to a fee. | Because your appointment was cancelled within 24 hours, our cancellation fee applies. |
| We did not receive your deposit; therefore your appointment has been released. | We didn't receive your deposit, so we've had to release your appointment time. We're sorry about that. |
| Your cancellation has been processed. | You're all cancelled. We hope to see you again soon. |
| We regret to inform you that your provider is unavailable. | We're sorry to let you know that Maya can't make it on Thursday. |

## Clients

1. **Greet them by first name.** Email: "Hi Ana," on its own line. Text: "Hi Ana, …". Every text is sent with the studio name in front ("Opal Nail Studio: Hi Ana, …", added in lib/sms.ts), so a text never names the studio again.
2. **Write in full sentences.** No fragments, slang or shorthand ("Heads up", "All set!", "FYI", "24h", "appt"). Write out numbers with units ("24 hours", "$32.50"), and days and times in full ("Wednesday, October 14 at 10:00 AM"; a text may shorten to "Wed, Oct 14 at 10:00 AM").
3. **Apologize when we caused it or something went wrong for them.** Do it once and sincerely: "We're sorry about this." / "We're sorry for any confusion." Don't apologize for the client's own choices.
4. **When a policy applies, say it once in plain words, then what it means for them, with their numbers.**
   > Because your appointment was cancelled 19 hours before it started, our cancellation fee of half the service price applies, which is $32.50. Your $20 deposit goes toward it, and we've charged the remaining $12.50 to your card on file.
5. **Never blame.** "We didn't receive your deposit", not "You didn't pay". "We weren't able to see you today", not "You missed your appointment".
6. **Say what happens next, and how to reach us** when they may want to ask: "If you have any questions, please reply to this message or call us at (555) 555-0100."
7. **Thank them where it's natural:** "Thank you for letting us know." / "Thank you for your patience."
8. **Close emails** with "Kind regards," and the studio name. Texts need no sign-off.
9. **Keep texts short but complete,** ideally under 320 characters. Put details in the email, not the text.
10. **Calm and certain.** At most one exclamation mark, only for good news. No emoji. No ALL CAPS. Prefer periods to dashes.
11. **Private.** Never mention another client, or why a staff member is away.

## Staff

Be clear, neutral and respectful: who, what, when, and what (if anything) they need to do.

> Ana Ruiz has cancelled her gel fill on Wednesday, October 14 at 10:00 AM. The time is now available.
> Action needed: Bo Lee's $20 deposit for Monday at 2:00 PM has not been received. Please contact her before her appointment.

No jokes, no slang, no blame ("Maya forgot to…"). Start with "Action needed:" only when something needs doing.

## Words to use

| Instead of | Write |
|---|---|
| Heads up | Please note |
| You're good | Your appointment is confirmed |
| Your time was released | We've had to release your appointment time |
| No-show | Missed appointment |
| Grab a time / snag a spot | Book a time |
| Hold on / hang tight | Thank you for your patience |
| ASAP | as soon as possible |
